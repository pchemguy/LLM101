---

## Slide 4 — What Is a Large Language Model?

LLMs are models trained on massive text datasets.

Core function:

> Predict the next token given previous tokens.

Capabilities:

* answering questions
* writing text
* coding
* summarization
* reasoning.

Important:

LLMs generate **probabilistically plausible text**, not guaranteed facts.

# 5. Tokens and Context

## Slide 8 — What Is a Token?

LLMs process **tokens**, not words.

Approximation:

* 1 token ≈ 4 characters
* ~0.75 English words.

Tokens determine:

* cost
* context limits
* processing capacity.

---

## Slide 9 — Context Window

The context window defines how much information the model can consider at once.

Includes:

* user prompt
* system instructions
* conversation history
* uploaded documents.

Typical ranges:

* 8k tokens → small models
* 100k+ tokens → large models.

# 11. AI Agents

## Slide 26 — What Are AI Agents?

Agents use LLMs as decision engines.

Agents could be compared, for example, to a research assistant who is given a goal ("find data on this"), goes to the library, realizes the book is checked out, and decides to look for a journal article instead.

Basic loop:

1. receive objective
2. plan actions
3. use tools
4. evaluate results
5. iterate.

Agents enable **automation of complex workflows**.

# 12. Limitations

## Slide 27 — Common Limitations

LLMs may:

* hallucinate facts
* produce incorrect citations
* make numerical errors
* misunderstand context.

Always verify outputs.

---

## Slide 28 — What LLMs Cannot Do Yet

LLMs cannot reliably:

* guarantee factual accuracy
* replace expert judgment
* design research independently
* ensure causal validity.

They are **assistants, not authorities**.

# 13. Common Mistakes
